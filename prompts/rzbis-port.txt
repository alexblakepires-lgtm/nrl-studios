CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, mounted high on the rim of an open-pit copper mine, looking down at a slight oblique.
LENS: Leica Summilux-C 40mm T1.4 stopped to T5.6, deep focus from rim to far benches.
LIGHT: Sun 6 degrees above the horizon at 3.100K raking from camera-right, lighting only the upper terraces. The lower pit falls into open shadow under 6.500K sky fill, two and a half stops below key. Sunlit rock at 90 IRE, deep pit at 12 IRE.
SUBJECT: Terraced benches spiraling down into the earth, exposed rock banded in rust, ochre and copper-green. Two small haul trucks climb the ramp with headlights on, tiny in the scale.
FOREGROUND: Loose ore and dust on the rim edge, soft, catching the last warm light.
MIDGROUND: Terraces sharp, fine dust haze hanging between the benches.
BACKGROUND: Far pit wall dissolving into blue-violet haze under a clear dusk sky.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: No people in frame. Truck paint weathered dull yellow.
MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Rock faces dry and fractured, copper oxide stains glinting where the low sun rakes them.
POST BEHAVIOR: Kodak Vision3 250D 5207, visible organic grain, halation on the sunlit rim, restrained saturation, warm highlights against cool shadow, midtone priority.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Pit spiral anchored lower-left, sunlit rim cutting a diagonal from top-right, trucks small in the lower third, broken symmetry.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
