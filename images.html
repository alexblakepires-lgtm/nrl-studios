FAQ

Good questions

Do they look like real photos?

Yes. We direct each image like a film shoot, choosing camera, lens, light and film grain, so they read as photography, not as "AI art."

Where can I use them?

On your website, social media, ads, menus, brochures and presentations.

Can the images show people?

Yes, people who look real but don't exist. We can't recreate you or any specific real person. If you want yourself in the shots, send us your own photos and we'll match the look around them.

What if I don't like them?

You get one round of changes on up to two images, with your notes. Most clients use it to adjust color or framing.

Can this become a full website?

That's often how it starts. The images become the visual language of your site, and we can build it from there.
